# Administrator guide

1. **Branches and staff.** Add locations under Branches. Create operator, inventory, accounts, driver or customer accounts under Staff. Link customer accounts to a customer record. Privileged staff need a provisioned authenticator secret.
2. **Products and stock.** Add a product, then an effective price under Products & pricing. Under Inventory, add a warehouse and record opening stock as a receipt. Stock transfers record paired movements. Submit a physical count under Stock reconciliation. A different authorized reviewer can approve a variance and post its ledger entry.
3. **Customers and connections.** Register a customer with an international-format mobile number. Add a connection with eligible products and refill limits. Record WhatsApp opt-in only when the customer has given permission.
4. **Bookings and deliveries.** Create a booking from an eligible connection. Confirm it, allocate stock, assign a driver and dispatch. Drivers record the recipient, filled units delivered and empty units collected. Branch settings can require a delivery code. The customer retrieves the six-digit code from the signed-in booking page and shares it at handover. The dispatch requires an active customer portal account. If delivery fails or the code expires, record the reason and physically returned filled units before dispatching another attempt.
5. **Collections.** The invoice is created when delivery is completed. Record cash or a verified UPI payment from the invoice or delivery. Print the booking and invoice from the booking detail. The outstanding amount updates as payments are recorded.
6. **Support.** Create and resolve tickets with progress notes. Safety tickets are urgent. Direct customers with immediate danger to emergency services from a safe location.
7. **WhatsApp.** Configure approved event-to-template mappings in Winquly settings. Use the communications page to inspect outbox states and retry dead-letter events once the cause is resolved.
